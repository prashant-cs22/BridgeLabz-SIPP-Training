Pseudo-code: Online Survey Report Generator

Class SurveyResponse
    Attributes: restaurantName, rating

Method generateReport(responses)
    Group responses by restaurantName
    For each group → calculate average rating

    Print each restaurant with average rating
    Identify highest and lowest rated

Example:
PizzaHut → [4, 5] → Avg = 4.5
KFC → [3, 4] → Avg = 3.5
Subway → [5, 4, 5] → Avg = 4.67

Output:
Highest: Subway (4.67)
Lowest: KFC (3.5)
