Pseudo-code: Simple Word Counter

Input: Text paragraph

Method wordCount(text)
    Convert text to lowercase
    Remove punctuation
    Split text into words
    For each word
        Update count in Map

    Print all words with frequency

Example:
"Sports bring people together. Sports inspire teamwork. Sports create leaders."

Output:
sports → 3
bring → 1
people → 1
together → 1
inspire → 1
teamwork → 1
create → 1
leaders → 1
